## Design notes & reasoning 💡

- Bottleneck: baseline does open + write + flush + os.fsync under a global lock for every request — heavy syscall and lock overhead under high concurrency.
- Key idea: keep the computation synchronous (same as baseline) but decouple and batch I/O with a background writer thread. Batch writes reduce the number of flush/fsync syscalls and remove lock contention on the hot path, producing much lower tail latency and higher throughput.
- Correctness: the optimized worker reuses `baseline_worker._slow_compute` and writes the same record fields (`job_id`, `payload_hash`, `length`, `vowels`, `start_ts`, `processed_ts`). run_tests validates log counts and that `length`/`vowels` match per job to ensure logical equivalence.
